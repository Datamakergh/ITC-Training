|Project 3: Photo Album
Remind trainees to use photos they are happy to share, not other people's private pictures. |Your Own App
Part 7 is where trainees put everything together: they choose their own idea, plan it as a small MVP, build it over four days and present it at a showcase.
|Your Own App
Explain the freedom, and the responsibility. They choose the idea, but they must finish and publish it in five days. Show the 5-day plan and stress Day 16: planning only, no code. Trainees who skip planning usually run out of time.
|Your Own App
Give trainees five minutes to answer the three questions on paper before they touch the computer. Then they use the brainstorming prompt. Encourage local, practical ideas, they are easier to explain at the showcase and more useful afterwards.
|Your Own App
Tell the skateboard story. Then walk through the church example and ask trainees to sort their own features into the three columns. Rule: Day 17 builds only the must-haves. If a must-have list has more than three items, it's too big.
|Your Own App
Every trainee runs the planning prompt and writes the plan in their notebook. Then they bring the plan to you for sign-off before any code. Check the four questions on the right. Pay attention to private data: an app that collects names and phone numbers needs much more care, steer them away from that for this project.
|Your Own App
Use this any day, it fits especially well on Days 17 and 18, when trainees work on their own. When a trainee hits a problem, don't fix it for them: ask the class what was missing from the prompt. The last row is a lifesaver: a long, messy chat often confuses the AI, and a fresh start fixes it.
|Wrap-Up
Give pairs five minutes. Then invite two or three trainees to show their page on the projector. Mention the publishing options for those who want a shareable link after the session.
|Wrap-Up
Congratulate the class, everyone built a portfolio, an expense tracker and a photo album today. Share next steps and take questions.
