Vibe coding has four steps. You describe the web page you want. The AI writes the code. You open it and test it. Then you ask for improvements. You do not need to learn the code. You are the boss. The AI is the builder. EXAMPLE: Like telling a builder 'I want a house with two bedrooms and a blue door.' You do not lay the bricks yourself.
Day 1Day 2Day 3Day 4Day 5
To build with AI, use the CLEAR recipe. Five letters. C = Context: what is it, and who is it for? L = Language: say 'HTML in one single file'. E = Elements: the parts of the page. A = Appearance: colours and style. R = Result: what it should do, for example look good on a phone. INTERPRETER: CLEAR is an English word that helps people remember. Explain each part; keep the letters in English.

Today you will build three real projects with AI, one after another. Project 1: a portfolio, a web page about you and your skills. Project 2: an expense tracker, an app that adds up your money. Project 3: a photo album, a gallery for your photos. These pictures are real pages, made with the same prompts you will use. Yours will look different, because you choose the words and colours. RULE: Start a NEW chat and a NEW file for each project.

Four steps for every project. 1. Copy the code: click the Copy button under the code. 2. Open Notepad and paste. 3. Save it with a name that ends in .html: portfolio.html, then expenses.html, then album.html. In 'Save as type', choose 'All Files'. 4. Double-click the file. It opens in your browser. That is your website! INTERPRETER: Show the steps on the projector while translating.

Project 1 is a portfolio: a web page that shows who you are, what you can do, and how to contact you. It is like a business card on the internet. It works 24 hours a day. Look at the example. It has five parts: a header with a menu, About me, Skills, Projects and Contact. IDEA: If you have a small business (tailoring, hair, food, phone repair), you can make the page about your business instead. SAFETY: Only a public email address. No phone number, no home address, no ID numbers. Note: the example is a real page made for this course; 'Amina' is an invented name.

Now improve your page. Talk to the AI in normal words. You do not need technical words. Ask for one change at a time. After each change, copy, save and open again to test it. EXAMPLES: 'Make the title bigger.' 'Change the background to light green.' 'Add a section with photos.' 'The button does nothing. Make it open WhatsApp.'

Project 2 is your first app that DOES things, not only shows things. Many families keep a small book of money in and money out. This is the same book on a computer, but it adds up by itself. Look at the example. You type a name and an amount, choose a type (Food, Transport, Bills), and press Add. The total updates by itself. The round chart shows where the money goes. How could this help you or your family? Note: the example is a real page made for this course.

Start a NEW chat for this project. Save the code as expenses.html. Fill in your currency and colours, then send the prompt. TEST: Add 3 expenses. Is the total right? Check with a calculator. Delete one: does the total go down? Press Add with an empty form: what happens? Refresh the page: where did your expenses go? (They disappear. The next slide fixes that.)

Now add three more features. Send ONE prompt, test it, then send the next. PROMPT 2, filter: shows only one type of spending, and its total. PROMPT 3, save: the expenses stay after you close the page. They are saved only on this computer's browser. PROMPT 4, chart: a round chart that shows where the money goes. TIP: If something breaks, say what you see and ask the AI to fix only that part.

Project 3 is a photo album: like the family albums people pass around, but on the computer. Look at the example: a neat grid of photos, a caption under each one, album names (Family, Friends, Travel), a search box and an Upload button. Click a photo to see it big. PRIVACY: Only use photos you are allowed to use. Ask people before you put their photo in, and do not put photos of other people's children online. Note: the example is a real page made for this course, with free photos from Unsplash.

New chat, new file: album.html. Send Prompt 1, test it, then send Prompt 2. Prompt 1 makes the grid with sample photos. Prompt 2 adds the 'lightbox': click a photo and it opens big on a dark screen, like the picture on the right. MORE (if there is time): 'Add an Upload button so I can add my own photos.' 'Add a search box that shows only photos whose caption has my words.' NOTE: Uploaded photos may disappear when you refresh. That is normal for practice.

Problems are normal. Here is what to do. Blank or broken page? Tell the AI what you see: 'The page is blank. What went wrong?' Not what you wanted? Describe it again, with more detail. The same error again and again? Start a new chat. Paste your last code that worked, and describe the problem. Ask yourself: what information was missing from my prompt?

PRACTICAL 7 · ABOUT 75 MINUTES · ON YOUR OWN Build the three projects, one after another. Start a new chat and a new file for each one. Portfolio: about 20 minutes. Expense tracker: about 25 minutes. Photo album: about 25 minutes. For each: fill in the prompt, send it, copy-save-open, then make at least one improvement. TRAINER: Walk around and help. When someone is stuck, ask: 'What did you tell the AI? What is missing?' Fast learners can do the extra prompts (filter, save, chart, upload, search). SAFETY: public email only; no home address; only photos you are allowed to use.
