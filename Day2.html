QUESTION 1: When the car is upside down in the air, can a person react fast enough? (No. Things happen in fractions of a second, so the car's computer must control the thrusters.) QUESTION 2: Automated or autonomous? (Good discussion! The stunt is planned and practised in a closed area: the computer follows a plan and keeps the car balanced, which is mostly AUTOMATED. AUTONOMOUS would mean deciding by itself in new, busy places, like a city. The engineer says it cannot do that.) QUESTION 3: Is it ready for the road? (No. Maybe production in 2028, and it must pass safety rules. Exciting videos are not the same as a finished product: always check the facts, as we learned in Part 4.) INTERPRETER: thrusters = small jets that push the car; production = when the company starts making and selling it. Source: NBC Nightly News report, supplied by the course organisers. Keep it for classroom use only.
